Common Questions

Helpful Answers For Parents

Quick answers to our most commonly asked administrative and campus care questions.

Morning drop-off begins at 7:30 AM with supervised breakfast play centers. Core academic programs run from 8:30 AM until 3:00 PM. Our extended day care and after-school enrichment clubs extend until 6:00 PM for working families.

Kiddle is a strictly nut-free facility. Our executive chef prepares custom dairy-free, gluten-free, and allergen-safe meals labeled specifically for each registered child, cross-checked by our on-site pediatric nurse.

For our Toddler Explorers program (1.5 - 2.5 yrs), potty training is not required! Our classrooms have private, child-scaled changing facilities and teachers gently collaborate with parents on potty training when your child shows developmental readiness.

We use the secure Kiddle Parent Mobile App. You receive real-time notifications with photos of class activities, nap logs, meal updates, and direct messaging with your child's lead educators.
