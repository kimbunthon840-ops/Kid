Our Educational Philosophy

Cultivating Wonder, Resilience & Kindness Every Single Day

Founded in 2008 by a council of passionate early childhood educators, Kiddle has grown into one of the region's most beloved preschools. We believe that learning in early years must never feel like forced instruction; rather, it should feel like an enchanting journey of discoveries.

Our hybrid curriculum harmonizes the best aspects of Montessori child-led exploration, Reggio Emilia project inquiry, and modern evidence-based social emotional development.

The Four Pillars

Holistic Early Childhood Framework

Every activity, song, and outdoor game is thoughtfully designed to develop the whole child.

Cognitive Acuity

Developing early mathematical logic, problem-solving habits, spatial understanding, and rich phonological awareness.

Social & Emotional

Teaching mindfulness, self-regulation, compassionate conflict resolution, and collaborative friendship.

Physical Motor Skills

Daily outdoor agility courses, sand play, gross motor balancing, and fine-motor scissor/clay exercises.

Creative Expression

Music rhythm ensembles, watercolor studios, imaginative theatrical role-play, and storytelling circles.

Safety & Compliance

Uncompromising Standards of Care

Your child's health, nutritional nourishment, and emotional security are protected at all times.

Hospital-Grade Sanitation

Air-purified classrooms with medical-grade HEPA filters, hourly touchpoint disinfection, and child-safe organic cleaners.

On-Site Pediatric Nurse

Full-time registered nurse on campus, with all faculty members certified in Pediatric CPR, First Aid, and EpiPen use.

CCTV & Biometric Access

24/7 high-definition security surveillance, perimeter fencing, electronic keycard gates, and strict guardian ID verification.
