What to Expect on Your 45-Minute Tour

We know finding the right early childhood academy is one of the most important milestones for your family. Here is how your visit is structured:

1. Welcome & Director Chat (15 Mins)

Enjoy fresh tea or organic coffee while our Director introduces our philosophy and answers your primary questions.

2. Live Class Observation (15 Mins)

See the teachers and children in their natural rhythm: circle song time, hands-on centers, and outdoor playground fun.

3. Welcome Kit & Enrollment Guide (15 Mins)

Receive an all-inclusive tuition schedule, admission checklist, and a colorful art kit for your child to take home.
